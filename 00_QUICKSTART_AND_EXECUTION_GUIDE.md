## 🛠️ The 5 Pillars in Action

### Pillar 1: Mental & Social Boundaries
* **Practice:** Open the **Mind & Boundaries** tab on the dashboard.
* **Feature:** Use the *Boundary Script Generator* to handle intrusive questions from relatives (*"When are you getting married?"*, salary comparisons, or late-night WhatsApp work pings).
* **Rule:** Enforce the *Digital Sunset*—phone placed on charge outside the bedroom 30 minutes before sleep.

### Pillar 2: Indian Wealth & Financial Safety (₹)
* **Practice:** Open the **Indian Wealth** tab.
* **Feature:** Input your in-hand monthly salary into the **50/30/20 Allocator** (50% Needs, 30% Lifestyle, 20% Automated SIPs).
* **Rule:** Use the **48-Hour Impulse Spend Pause** for any non-essential purchase above ₹1,000 (prevents Blinkit, Zepto, and flash-sale leaks).

### Pillar 3: Physical Vitality & High-Protein Fuel
* **Practice:** Open the **Physical Vitality** tab.
* **Feature:** Enter your body weight to calculate your exact daily protein requirement (1.3g/kg) and view targeted food stacks (Paneer, Tofu, Soya chunks, Curd, Eggs, Whey).
* **Rule:** Morning balcony sunlight (15 mins) + 8,000 steps + dinner finished before 8:15 PM.

### Pillar 4: 90-Minute Career Moat
* **Practice:** Open the **Career Moat** tab.
* **Feature:** Start the built-in **90-Minute Deep Work Timer**. Log what you built or learned today that cannot be easily automated by AI.

### Pillar 5: 24-Hour Daily Urban Rhythm
* **Practice:** Open the **24h Urban Rhythm** tab.
* **Feature:** Follow the structured morning wake-up and evening wind-down checklists designed for Indian traffic, metro commutes, and busy workdays.

## 🖨️ How to Export the 52-Page Master Planner to PDF

1. Double-click or open **[dear_2027_master_planner_52pages.html](file:///c:/Users/Asus/Downloads/ebook/dear_2027_master_planner_52pages.html)** in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
2. In the top-right toolbar, click **"🖨️ Print / Save as A4 PDF"** (or press `Ctrl + P`).
3. Set **Destination** to **"Save as PDF"**.
4. Set **Paper size** to **A4**.
5. Set **Margins** to **None** (the template has built-in 22mm binding edge margins and 16mm outer margins).
6. Enable the checkbox **"Background graphics"**.
7. Click **Save**. You now have a complete, publication-grade 52-page PDF file ready for digital note-taking or commercial distribution on SuperProfile.
