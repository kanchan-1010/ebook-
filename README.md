## 📖 Master Deliverables Breakdown

### 1. Master Portal & Launchpad (`index.html`)
* One-click navigation hub connecting all components of the project.
* Directly open the interactive dashboard, view or print the 52-page journal, or read the underlying research and marketing guides.

### 2. Daily Life Reset Operating System Web App (`life_reset_dashboard.html`)
* **Pillar 1:** Indian family boundary scripts (marriage questions, salary inquiries, after-hours work pings) + digital detox protocol.
* **Pillar 2:** Live 50/30/20 Indian Rupee (₹) salary allocator, 6-month emergency buffer calculator, and 48-hour impulse-spend cooling timer.
* **Pillar 3:** Indian diet protein macro calculator (Veg, Eggetarian, Non-Veg) with targeted daily food stacks (Paneer, Tofu, Soya chunks, Curd, Eggs, Whey) + water and step trackers.
* **Pillar 4:** Built-in 90-minute / 45-minute Career Moat focus timer with deep work logging.
* **Pillar 5:** 24-hour Indian urban daily rhythm + morning & evening shutdown routines.
* **Storage:** Seamlessly persists all user inputs across browser sessions using `localStorage`.

### 3. Master 52-Page Journal & A4 Printable (`dear_2027_master_planner_52pages.html`)
* Complete, publication-grade layout styled in Warm Cream (`#FFF8EF`), Sage Green (`#A8B89A`), and Muted Gold (`#D6B56D`).
* Features generous 22mm left binding margins (for physical spiral binding) and 16mm outer margins.
* Covers all 52 pages: 2026 Reflection, 8-Spoke Wheel of Life, Vision Board, 12 Dated 2027 Monthly Spreads, and Quarterly Reviews.
* Built-in toolbar with **"🖨️ Print / Save as A4 PDF"**, sample data filler, and blank mode toggle.

### 4. Full Text Manuscript (`02_COMPLETE_PLANNER_CONTENT_52_PAGES.md`)
* The raw text content of every single page (Pages 1 to 52) with guided questions, author letters, and disclaimers.

### 5. Visual Identity & Canva Prompts (`03_DESIGN_SYSTEM_AND_PAGE_PROMPTS.md`)
* Complete brand style guide with hex colors, typography specifications, and 52 individual copy-pasteable design prompts for Canva or Midjourney.

### 6. Sales Launch & Marketing Playbook (`05_MARKETING_AND_SALES_LAUNCH_PLAYBOOK.md`)
* Ready-to-copy SuperProfile product listing description, 10 viral Instagram reel scripts, 5 carousel blueprints, and unit sales break-even targets.

## 🚀 How to Run & Use the Project
* **One-Click Launch:** Double-click `start_preview.bat` or run `python start_server.py` to open the Master Portal in your default browser.
* **Open Master Hub Directly:** Double-click `index.html` to open in any browser.
* **Directly Access the 52-Page PDF:** Open `dear_2027_master_planner_52pages.pdf` (ready for upload to SuperProfile or print).
* **Re-compile the 52-Page Journal HTML:** Run `python generate_master_planner_html.py`.
* **Re-export to PDF:** Run `python export_planner_to_pdf.py`.
