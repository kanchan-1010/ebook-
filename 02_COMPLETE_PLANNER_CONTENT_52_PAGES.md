  2. **Clear one physical surface:** Clean just your study desk or make your bed (takes 3 minutes).
  3. **Pick ONE micro-task:** Do not try to catch up on everything today. Complete one single 10-minute task.
  4. **Forgive yourself in ink:** Write below: *"I am allowed to have messy days. My commitment resumes today."*
