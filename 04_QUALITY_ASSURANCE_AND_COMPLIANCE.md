* **Risk Identified:** Recommending investment allocations (e.g., SIPs, Emergency Funds) without a license could conflict with SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India) Investment Advisers Regulations.
* **Resolution Implemented:** Explicitly featured on Page 2:
  *"The financial templates and 50/30/20 guidelines in this journal are purely for personal educational and organizational purposes. The author is not a SEBI-registered financial advisor. Readers must exercise their own discretion or consult certified financial planners before making investment decisions."*

### 3.2 Mental Wellness Disclaimer
* **Risk Identified:** Self-reflection exercises on burnout or anxiety being misconstrued as psychological therapy.
* **Resolution Implemented:** Explicitly featured on Page 2:
  *"This journal provides guided self-reflection and lifestyle organization. It does not constitute formal medical advice, psychotherapy, or clinical counseling. Individuals dealing with severe clinical distress should consult a licensed healthcare professional."*

* **Fonts:** All recommended fonts (`Playfair Display`, `Cormorant Garamond`, `Lora`, `Montserrat`, `Inter`) are licensed under the **SIL Open Font License (OFL)**, permitting free commercial digital and print distribution.
